Orthodox Bible & Calendar

The Orthodox calendar of your own church, the Septuagint Bible and the daily prayers.

Support

Write to us at filiptr@yahoo.com, or use Settings → Send feedback in the app. If your question is about a purchase, include the support ID shown at the bottom of Settings.

Premium

To restore a purchase on a new phone, open Settings → Premium and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account. To manage or cancel a subscription, open your Apple Account settings, then Subscriptions.

Apple Watch

Install the watch app from the Watch app on your iPhone, then open Orthodox Bible & Calendar on your iPhone once to bring the Church days to your watch.
