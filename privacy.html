Privacy Policy

Orthodox Bible & Calendar · Last updated October 22, 2026 · Contact: filiptr@yahoo.com

Orthodox Bible & Calendar is made to be used without giving anything away. There is no account to create, no advertising, and no tracking across apps or websites. The app is published by Filip Trajkovski.

What stays on your phone

Everything you do in the app is kept only on your device: your church and calendar, saved verses and quotes, notes, pinned prayers, reading plans, your prayer rule and prayer rope counts, name days, the names you commemorate, your reminder times, and where you stopped listening. We never receive any of it. Deleting the app deletes it.

The app makes a random anonymous number when it is first opened (the support ID in Settings). It identifies no one; it only lets us find a purchase or a message you have sent, if you ask us to.

Listening

Recordings are streamed from, or downloaded to your phone from, our storage provider, Supabase. Like any web server, it records technical details of each request, such as your IP address, which we use only to keep the service running. Downloaded recordings stay on your phone, and are kept out of your iCloud and device backups.

What you may choose to send

The community email list. If you choose to join it, we store your email address with the app's anonymous number, your calendar choice (Old or New), your phone's platform, and the app's name. We use them only to send our newsletter. You can unsubscribe from any email in one tap, or ask us to delete your address. We store it with Supabase and never sell or share it.

Feedback. If you send feedback from Settings, we receive your message, the email address you choose to add (if any, so that we can reply), the app's version, your phone's system and version, your church and calendar, and the app's anonymous number. We use them only to answer you and improve the app, store them with Supabase, and never sell or share them.

Purchases. If you buy Premium, Apple handles the payment; we never see your card. Our purchase provider, RevenueCat, receives the app's anonymous number and your purchase receipts, so that Premium works on your devices. After buying you may add your email; it is kept with RevenueCat only so that we can find your purchase if something goes wrong, and we send nothing to it.

Apple Search Ads. If you came to the app from one of our Apple Search Ads, Apple's own attribution (AdServices) tells RevenueCat which ad campaign it was. This doesn't track you across other apps or websites, and needs no permission.

Your Apple Watch, widgets and Photos

The app sends the Church day and your prayer rope counts between your iPhone and your Apple Watch directly; nothing passes through our servers. Widgets show what the app has prepared on your phone. If you save a watch face, the app asks only to add it to Photos; it never reads your photos.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone itself. Nothing is sent to a server to deliver them.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see or delete what we hold about you: your community email, your feedback messages, or the email on a purchase. Write to filiptr@yahoo.com; including the support ID from Settings helps us find it.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It isn't directed at children under 13, and collects nothing from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
